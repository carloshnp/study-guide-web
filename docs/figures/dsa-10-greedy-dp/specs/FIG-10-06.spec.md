# FIG-10-06: Exercises in order
status: draft   rev: 1   date: 2026-10-22   author: illustration spec agent

## 1. Summary
One square flat diagram of the exercise section of chapter 10, read left to right as three columns under three headings: Greedy arguments, Tables by hand, Memoized vs tabulated. Each column holds three small exercise cards, drawn as blank base-outline cards, and each card has an accent bar along its top. Accent arrows run from the first column to the second and from the second to the third. One card in the middle column is a warning-role card, a wrong answer kept as a contrast, marked with the note "table not checked". A footer strip under the columns reads, in three labels, argument first, table second, compare last. No problem statement, no answer, no number and no code is drawn. The picture shows the order of the work, and the ledger in section 4 says what the chapter supports.
MESSAGE: Work the exercises in order: the argument first, the hand table second, and the comparison of the two styles last.

## 3. Goals and non-goals
- GOAL-1: the three headings name the three groups in reading order, left to right, with no legend.
- GOAL-2: the two accent arrows show the order between groups, from the first column to the second and from the second to the third.
- GOAL-3: the middle column's second card is the only warning-role element, and its note "table not checked" says it is a wrong answer kept for contrast.
- GOAL-4: the footer strip repeats the order of work in three labels, each under its column.
- GOAL-5: each card carries one accent bar, so every exercise reads as a unit with the same marking.
- NON-GOAL-1: problem statements, answers, numbers and any digit. No card shows legible text.
- NON-GOAL-2: the count of problems per group. The chapter has five, six and five; the figure draws three cards per column and states no count.
- NON-GOAL-3: a comma-separated footer sentence. The footer is three labels, because the full sentence is longer than the twenty-four character limit.
- NON-GOAL-4: any code, table of values, or grading mark beyond the warning card.

## 8. Composition plan
- DOMINANT: the three columns of cards read as one row of work.
- FOCAL MECHANISM: three of six. Hue contrast: the warning card against the white cards. Convergence: the two accent arrows lead the eye across the columns. Isolation: the warning card is the only card with a heavy outline.
- HIERARCHY: three levels. Level one, the three columns and their cards. Level two, the two accent arrows and the warning card. Level three, the headings and the footer strip.
- READING PATH: first heading, first column, arrow, second heading, second column, warning card, arrow, third column, footer strip.
- FRAMING: the plate fills the square; the columns start below the headings at top twenty-two percent and end above the footer strip; the third column is wider, because its heading is the longest.
- NEGATIVE SPACE: the band between the cards and the footer is empty, so the footer reads as a separate step.
- GROUPING: cards sit inside their column panel, with more space between columns than between cards, so each column reads as one group.
