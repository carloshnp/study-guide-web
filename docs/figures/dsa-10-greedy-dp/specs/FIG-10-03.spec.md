# FIG-10-03: Argument header above the listing
status: draft
rev: 1   date: 2026-10-22   author: Hermes Agent, drafted for the requester

## 1. Summary
The figure shows the argument header of a greedy listing. A tall listing card holds three header strips, Choice rule, Exchange argument and Measure, in that order. Each strip has an arrow to the body bar it governs. Below the header, abstract body bars carry no legible text, and two of them are marked checked before run. A side note says the argument is written before the code. The figure is a flat layout sketch for Seedream; it draws no code and no table.
MESSAGE: The argument sits above the listing, so no step runs without its reason on the page.

## 2. Communicative intent
- AUDIENCE: an exam-revising student who has met stacks, queues, heaps and graphs (context only; not a sourced fact).
- INTENDED CHANGE: understand that the greedy argument is written first and sits above the listing; do: read the header before running the steps.
- READING CONTEXT: study-site chapter 10, section 10.3 (anchor 103-argument-first-specs-and-reconstruction), beside the argument-first sentence and the header paragraph (ledger rows, section 4). Light theme is drawn. Dark theme tokens are not in BRIEF and are not drawn in this pass (Q5). Read in the lightbox on a phone: the strip labels must hold at a narrow column width (REQ-FIG-11).
- REPRODUCTION: square, one to one raster; shown at text-column width beside section 10.3.1.
- LIFETIME: one chapter; part of the chapter 10 set that shares the BRIEF palette and line grammar.
- ASSUMPTIONS: one to four are inherited from the confirmed list (PLAN.md assumptions one to seventeen; assumption four is superseded by A6) plus A5 to A8 (BRIEF detail rewrite). N1 to N4 are new and pending requester confirmation.
  1. Camera, light and aspect as PLAN.md assumptions one, two and seven: flat, orthographic, no light source, square. (inherited)

## 3. Goals and non-goals
- GOAL-1: the three header strips read top to bottom as Choice rule, Exchange argument, Measure, inside one card.
- GOAL-2: each strip has one arrow to one body bar, and no arrow reaches a checked bar.
- GOAL-3: two body bars are marked checked before run by a warning outline, a bracket and one label.
- GOAL-4: a side note outside the card states that the argument is written before the code.
- GOAL-5: the card reads as one tall card with the body below the header.
- NON-GOAL-1: legible code or code-like text in any bar.
- NON-GOAL-2: the DP header, its recurrence or its fill order (FIG-10-04 territory).
- NON-GOAL-3: a reconstruction walk or any table.
- NON-GOAL-4: the sort, the scan and the complexity bounds of the listing.

- REQ-FIG-02 [MUST-LOCAL] Each strip has one arrow leaving its right edge and ending at one connected body bar, and no arrow ends at a checked bar. (why: the governs mapping)

## 8. Composition plan
- DOMINANT: the three accent header strips, A-03, A-05 and A-07.
- FOCAL MECHANISM: value contrast (accent outline and accent-soft fill against the wash card); hue contrast (accent against muted and warning); size (the strips are the largest outlined marks in the card).
- HIERARCHY (three levels): one, the three header strips; two, the two checked bars with their bracket and label; three, card, body bars, arrows and side note.
- READING PATH: entry at argument header; second the Choice rule strip; third the arrows down into the body; payoff at the checked bracket and the side note.
- BALANCE: asymmetric; the card is on the left, the side column on the right.
- FRAMING: eight percent margin on the left and top; the card ends at ninety-six percent of the height.
- NEGATIVE SPACE: the open band below the body bars and the empty side column between the note and the checked label keep the note and the label as the only side marks.
- GROUPING: each arrow sits closest to its own strip and body bar.
