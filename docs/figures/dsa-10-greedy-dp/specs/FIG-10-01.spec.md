# FIG-10-01: Greedy exchange step
status: draft   rev: 1   date: 2026-10-22   author: illustration spec agent

## 1. Summary
One square flat diagram of a single exchange step on a shared timeline. The upper row is an optimal solution of four job bars. Its first bar, job i, is the one the swap removes. The lower row keeps the three other bars unchanged and puts job j, the earliest-finishing job, into the first slot. A bracket shows that job j ends before the next bar starts, a band on the right shows that both rows keep four bars, and a strip at the bottom states the verdict, still feasible. No values are drawn. The picture carries the shape of the argument; every claim it makes is in the ledger in section 4.
MESSAGE: Taking the earliest-finishing job is safe only because swapping it into an optimal solution keeps the solution feasible and just as large.

## 3. Goals and non-goals
- GOAL-1: the swapped job is named by the label on the accent bar, job j, and the removed job by the label on the warning bar, job i, with no legend.
- GOAL-2: the swap arrow runs straight down from the first slot of the upper row to the first slot of the lower row.
- GOAL-3: the gap between the job j bar and the next bar is marked by a bracket labelled no overlap.
- GOAL-4: the same size band shows that the lower row has as many bars as the upper row.
- GOAL-5: the verdict strip states that the swapped solution is still feasible.
- NON-GOAL-1: weights, values and the weighted case in which the greedy rule fails. No value appears in any form.
- NON-GOAL-2: repeated swaps and the stays-ahead argument. The figure shows one swap only.
- NON-GOAL-3: a table of jobs, code, a time axis or any digit.
- NON-GOAL-4: a worked answer to copy. The figure does not list which jobs are chosen.

## 8. Composition plan
- DOMINANT: the two job rows with the swap arrow between them.
- FOCAL MECHANISM: three of six. Hue contrast: accent and warning against the wash and the plate. Size: the job j bar is shorter than the job i bar, so it visibly finishes first. Convergence: the swap arrow lands on the job j bar.
- HIERARCHY: three levels. Level one, the two job rows and the swap arrow. Level two, the no-overlap bracket and the same size band. Level three, the captions and the verdict strip.
- READING PATH: exchange step caption, upper row (job i), swap arrow, lower row (job j), no-overlap bracket, result arrow, verdict strip. The same size band is read alongside both rows.
- FRAMING: the plate fills the square; content runs from the left margin to the right band and from the top caption to the verdict strip.
- NEGATIVE SPACE: the right of the upper caption and the bottom strip of the square stay empty. The gap between the rows holds only the swap arrow, so the eye travels one line down.
- GROUPING: base bars share columns in both rows, so the eye compares the rows column by column.
