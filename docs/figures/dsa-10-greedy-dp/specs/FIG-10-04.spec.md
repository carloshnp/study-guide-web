# FIG-10-04: Same recurrence, top down and bottom up
status: draft   rev: 1   date: 2026-10-22   author: illustration spec agent

## 1. Summary
One square flat diagram of one recurrence in two styles, split by a vertical divider. The left half is the top down style: a chain of three call boxes, each reading best(j), with arrows from each box to the box below, enclosed in a dashed loop labelled cache with the note reached states only. The right half is the bottom up style: a row of six cells filled left to right, an arrow labelled fill order, and the note all entries filled. The chain and the row are layout sketches: the number of boxes and cells carries no claim, and no value is drawn. Every claim the picture makes is in the ledger in section 4.
MESSAGE: One recurrence can run top down through a cache that visits only reached states, or bottom up through a table that fills every entry in order, and the access pattern decides which costs less.

## 3. Goals and non-goals
- GOAL-1: the reader sees three call boxes, each reading best(j), with an arrow from each box to the box below, and needs no legend to read the direction.
- GOAL-2: the reader sees that the dashed loop named cache holds the calls, and the note reached states only says what the loop keeps.
- GOAL-3: the reader sees one row of cells filled left to right, with the arrow named fill order and the note all entries filled.
- GOAL-4: the reader tells the two styles apart from the divider and the two panel labels top down and bottom up.
- NON-GOAL-1: no value, no call count, no state count and no cell value; the three boxes and six cells are sketch counts only.
- NON-GOAL-2: no second call best(p(j)) is drawn; the chain shows only the best(j-1) call that F18 names.
- NON-GOAL-3: no code text, no reconstruction path and no chosen set.
- NON-GOAL-4: no time or cost figure, and no claim that either style is faster in every case.

## 8. Composition plan
- DOMINANT: the cell row in view B. It holds the only accent mark, and the fill arrow lands on it.
- FOCAL MECHANISM: two of six. Hue contrast: the accent outline on the first cell against the wash cells. Convergence: the fill arrow runs into the row.
- HIERARCHY: three levels. Level one, the cell row and its fill arrow. Level two, the chain inside the dashed loop, and the divider. Level three, the panel labels and the two notes.
- READING PATH: top down label, call box 1, arrow, call box 2, arrow, call box 3, cache label, reached states only note; across the divider to the bottom up label, the fill order arrow, cell 1 to cell 6, then the all entries filled note.
- BALANCE: symmetric halves of equal height, with the left a column of calls and the right a row of cells.
- FRAMING: the plate fills the square; content runs from six percent to ninety-six percent of the width, with the top labels at eight percent.
- NEGATIVE SPACE: the lower right region below the cell row stays empty except the all entries filled note; the gaps between boxes hold only the arrows.
- GROUPING: the boxes and the loop form one group; the six cells form one row with equal gaps.
