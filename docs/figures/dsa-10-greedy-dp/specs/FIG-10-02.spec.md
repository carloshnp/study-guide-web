# FIG-10-02: Knapsack table fills row by row
status: draft

## 1. Summary

A square layout sketch of a knapsack table: five equal-height item rows over eight capacity columns. One cell in the lowest row is filled with the accent colour and labelled K(i, c). A bracket outlines the full row directly above it. Two arrows enter the cell: a straight arrow from the cell above, labelled skip item i, and a diagonal arrow from the cell above and to its left, labelled take item i. A label in the left margin names the row above, and a strip under the grid states that an entry is final when written. The figure shows the dependency pattern and no values. Exact cell values belong on the SVG path, which PLAN marks as recommended.

Deviation from the hint: the strip sits beneath the grid, not at the side, because the BRIEF grid spans to ninety percent of the width (OQ-A).

MESSAGE: A knapsack row is final once written because it reads only the row above it, so the last cell holds the answer.

## 7. Requirements

- REQ-FIG-01 [MUST-LOCAL] the grid has five rows of equal height, and the lowest row holds exactly one accent-filled cell (why: the entry under study is the one filled cell)
- REQ-FIG-02 [MUST-LOCAL] exactly two arrows end on the accent cell and both start in the row directly above it: one vertical from the cell in the same column, labelled skip item i; one diagonal from the cell one column to the left, labelled take item i (why: F09, the skip and take branches)
- REQ-FIG-03 [MUST-LOCAL] the accent bracket outline spans the full width of the fourth row and no other row carries an outline (why: F10, the row above is the only input)
- REQ-FIG-04 [MUST-LOCAL] the label row above sits in the left margin, with its vertical centre inside the bracketed row (why: amendment A2)
- REQ-FIG-05 [MUST-LOCAL] eight capacity columns are visible as seven thin vertical rules inside the grid, evenly spaced (why: capacity columns, hint)
- REQ-FIG-06 [MUST-LOCAL] exactly seven labels are drawn, each from the closed list, each once (why: A5, closed list)
- REQ-FIG-07 [MUST-LOCAL] no digit appears in any label, and no cell contains a number or a symbol (why: assumption 9)
- REQ-FIG-08 [MUST-LOCAL] every label's capital height is at least 1.6 percent of the square width (why: site text floor, declared local)
- REQ-FIG-09 [MUST-STANDARD] text reaches 4.5 to 1 against its surface; the accent outline and the arrows reach 3 to 1 against the card (source: WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11)
- REQ-FIG-10 [MUST-STANDARD] the accent cell is marked by fill, outline and label, and the two arrows differ by direction and label, so no meaning rests on hue alone (source: WCAG 1.4.1)
- REQ-FIG-11 [MUST-LOCAL] the sheet is flat: rectangles, straight lines and arrows only, with no gradient, shadow, light source or perspective (why: convention, assumptions 1, 2, 5)
- REQ-FIG-12 [MUST-LOCAL] the strip beneath the grid carries one line of text, "final when written", and the text sits inside the strip (why: A5, closed list; N-1)
- REQ-FIG-13 [SHOULD] the gap between the lowest row and the strip is at least 4 percent of the square height, so the strip reads as a separate band; cost: the grid cannot grow taller than declared (why: keeps B-01 apart from the grid, declared local)

## 8. Composition plan

| field | value |
|---|---|
| DOMINANT | the accent cell K(i, c) in the lowest row |
| FOCAL MECHANISM | three: isolation (the only filled cell), hue contrast (accent on white), convergence (two arrows meet the cell) |
| HIERARCHY | three levels: 1 the accent cell and its two arrows; 2 the bracketed row and the row-above label; 3 the rows, rules and remaining labels |
| READING PATH | arrows: from the row-above label to the bracketed row, then the two input arrows into the accent cell, then the strip, which says final when written |
| BALANCE | asymmetric; the mass sits low and right around the accent cell, and the left margin carries the item and row-above labels |
| FRAMING | the whole grid inside the square, a one percent plate frame, no crop |
| NEGATIVE SPACE | the empty card cells to the right of the accent cell in the lowest row keep the eye on the cell and its inputs; no order is implied across them |
| GROUPING | rows grouped by equal height and separators; the strip grouped as one frame |
